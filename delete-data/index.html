Delete some of your Wearly data

Wearly is made by Softilox Innovations Inc. You can delete specific data without deleting your account.

In the app

  • A clothing item and its photo: Closet → open the item → trash icon.
  • An event: Plan → Events → open it → Delete.
  • A trip: Plan → Trips → open it → trash icon.
  • Birthday, gender, fit and style preferences: You → About you → change or clear them.
  • Location: turn off location for Wearly in your phone's settings, and clear Home city under You.

By email

To ask us to delete other data (for example your outfit history, usage history or feedback messages), email support.wearly@gmail.com from your account's email address and tell us what to delete. We'll do it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What may be kept

Purchase records may be kept for up to 7 years where tax or accounting law requires. To delete everything, see Delete your account.
