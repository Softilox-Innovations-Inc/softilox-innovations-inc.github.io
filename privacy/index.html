Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how Softilox Innovations Inc. ("we") handles information in the Wearly mobile app ("the app"). Contact: support.wearly@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash), and optionally your first name. You confirm your email with a one-time code before you can use the app.
  • About you (optional): your birth date (to tailor looks to your age and show your zodiac sign), gender, preferred fit (for example relaxed or slim) and style preferences. You can change or clear these anytime under You → About you.
  • Clothing photos: photos you choose to add, the cropped garment photos we create from them, and the tags describing each item (category, colors, pattern, occasions, and fit and cut such as slim, baggy or wide-leg).
  • Approximate location: when you allow it, your coarse location while you use the app, to fetch local weather. We store your nearest city and rounded coordinates. We do not track location in the background.
  • Calendar events: only if you tap "Sync calendar" and grant permission. We read upcoming event titles, times and locations on your device and save only events that have a dress code (for example "wedding" or "interview").
  • Usage data: outfits you log, likes and dislikes, streaks, time spent in the app per day, the number of AI scans you use, and basic in-app events (such as opening the app or tapping a shopping suggestion). We use this to improve recommendations, apply plan limits, prevent abuse and keep AI costs fair.
  • Security data: failed sign-in attempts and the IP address of requests, kept briefly to lock accounts after repeated wrong passwords and to rate-limit abuse.
  • Community posts (only if you share): the photo, your caption and your first name, which every Wearly user can see, plus likes, comments and reports. Comments show your first name to other users, are checked automatically before they appear, and can be deleted by you or by the owner of the post. Location and camera details are removed from the photo before it is stored. Each photo is checked automatically (by Anthropic's Claude) for safety and to recognize the clothes for "shop similar" links. Photos that break the rules are not stored; we keep a record that a post was removed and why, and a flag on the account.
  • Profile and following: an optional profile photo, the people you follow, who follows you (shown as counts), and posts you save. Saved posts and the people you follow are private to you.
  • Looks and closet details: outfits you save as looks and their folders, the days you wore each look (wear calendar), your wishlist, and optional price, brand and size on closet items, used to work out cost per wear. Only you can see them.
  • Rate my look and "Should I buy this?": a photo you take for an outfit check, or of something you are thinking of buying, is sent to Anthropic for analysis and is not stored. We count how many checks you use each day to apply plan limits.
  • Studio photos (optional): when you turn on a studio background, the new backdrop is made on your device; the finished photo is stored like your other closet photos.
  • Support chat: messages you send to the Wearly team in the app and our replies.
  • Points and referrals: your points balance and history, your referral code, and who referred you or whom you referred.
  • Friends you style (optional): the name, age, style and sizes you enter for someone you build looks for. Only you can see them.
  • Try-on photos: a full-body photo of yourself that you choose to upload for virtual try-on, after confirming you are an adult and that the photo is of you, and the try-on images made from it. They are private to your account.
  • Device activity: when signed in, the app sends a random installation ID, device model, app version, available install source, and recent foreground activity to Wearly for account support.
  • App journey (iPhone and Android): from the first open, the app records which setup steps were reached (for example sign-up form, email code, plan screen, subscription) with a random installation ID, platform, app version, phone model, system version and the phone's region setting, so we can see where people have trouble. It includes no names or typed text, and is linked to your account once you sign in.
  • Apple Search Ads attribution (iPhone): at first launch the app asks Apple's AdServices for a one-time token, which our server exchanges with Apple to learn whether the install came from an Apple Search Ads campaign (campaign, ad group and keyword numbers). No tracking permission or advertising ID is used.
  • Android install attribution: after the app opens, Wearly may receive Google Play campaign labels, install and referral-click times, and the original app version with a random installation ID to measure acquisition. Wearly uses the installation ID to measure when the signup form opens and whether a new account is created and verified. Campaign reports do not show email addresses. Wearly does not send the raw referrer or ad click identifiers.
  • Push notifications: a notification token for your device, so we can send support replies and news. You can turn notifications off in Android settings.
  • Purchases: subscription status and one-time purchases (such as extra AI scan packs) from Google Play, via RevenueCat. We never see or store your card details; Google Play handles payment.

How we use it

To provide the app's features (your closet, daily outfit suggestions, weather-aware styling, event and trip planning, notifications), to run subscriptions, to prevent abuse, and to understand how the app is used in aggregate. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and private photo storage (hosted in the United States).
  • Google (Gmail): sends sign-up and password-reset codes to your email.
  • Anthropic: analyzes the clothing photos you add to detect and tag garments, checks community posts, comments and try-on photos. Photos are sent for analysis only; under Anthropic's commercial terms they are not used to train its models.
  • FASHN AI: creates try-on images from your try-on photo and a garment photo.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers push notifications to your device.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play: subscriptions and payments.
  • Open-Meteo and BigDataCloud: weather forecasts and city lookup from coordinates.

Shopping links

"Complete your wardrobe" opens retailer websites (such as Amazon) through affiliate links. We may earn a commission from qualifying purchases. We record that a link was tapped; we do not receive your purchase details from the retailer.

Our website

Our website (this site) uses no cookies and no third-party analytics or advertising trackers. To understand which pages are useful, it records anonymous page views and clicks: the page, the referring website, campaign tags in the link, and the approximate country, device type, browser and operating system. We do not store your IP address or browser details; visits are counted with an identifier derived from them that changes every day and cannot be traced back to you. Nothing is recorded if your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. This data is kept for up to 400 days.

Retention and deletion

Accounts whose email address is never verified and that were never signed in to are deleted automatically 60 days after they were created.

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (You → Delete account). This permanently deletes your profile, photos (closet, profile, try-on and community), looks, wishlist, follows, saved posts, garments, posts, outfits, events, trips, points, support messages and usage history. Deleting a single post deletes its photo. Billing records may be kept as required by law. You can also email us to request deletion. You can also download a copy of your closet, looks and wear history anytime (You → Export my data).

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Closet and try-on photos are stored privately; only your account can open them, through links that expire. Community photos you share are visible to signed-in Wearly users through links that expire after an hour. Uploads are checked on our servers, sign-in is protected by an account lock after repeated wrong passwords, and access controls ensure each user can read only their own data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data. Community photos that show children or teens are removed automatically, and try-on is for adults only.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to processing. Contact us at the email above.

Changes
