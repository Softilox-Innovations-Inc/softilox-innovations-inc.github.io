FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Wearly?

Wearly is an AI outfit planner and digital wardrobe app for iPhone and Android. You photograph your clothes once, and Wearly suggests complete outfits every day based on your weather, your plans and your style. It also plans outfits for events and trips and shows how your clothes would look on with virtual try-on.

Is Wearly free?

Yes, Wearly is free to download and the free plan lets you build your closet and get daily outfit ideas. Wearly Pro unlocks unlimited items, more AI scans, virtual try-ons and advanced planning, and you can try it free for 7 days.

How does the AI outfit planner work?

When you add a photo, AI finds each clothing item, crops it into its own natural photo and tags its category, colour, pattern, warmth and formality. Outfit matching then runs on your phone: it applies colour theory, checks today's forecast and the occasion, and avoids repeating what you wore recently.

Are my photos private?

Yes. Your closet photos are stored in private storage that only your account can access, and they are never shown to other people unless you choose to post a look to the community. You can delete your account and all of your data at any time from the app.

Does Wearly work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Wearly is available on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android phones, and your closet syncs when you sign in with the same account.

How does virtual try-on work?

Pick an outfit and a model photo, and Wearly generates a realistic image of the clothes worn together, so you can see the full look before you get dressed or buy something new.

Can I cancel the free trial?

Yes. You can cancel any time before the trial ends in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings and you won't be charged. Wearly also reminds you before the trial ends.
