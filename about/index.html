We think you already own enough clothes.

Wearly is made by Softilox Innovations Inc., a small team in Windsor, Ontario, Canada.

Most people wear a small share of what's in their closet, then buy more because getting dressed feels like a puzzle every morning. We built Wearly to solve that puzzle with the clothes you already have.

What we believe

  • Style is a skill, not a budget. Good outfits come from colour, proportion and fit, which anyone can learn and an app can help with.
  • Your closet is private. Photos live in private storage, outfit matching runs on your phone, and you can delete everything at any time.
  • Less waste is better style. Seeing cost per wear and the pieces you never reach for helps you buy less and choose better.

About the style journal

Our guides are written by the Wearly Style Team and reviewed against long-standing tailoring and dress-code conventions. We update them when advice changes, and we never accept payment for recommendations. If you spot a mistake, email support.wearly@gmail.com.
