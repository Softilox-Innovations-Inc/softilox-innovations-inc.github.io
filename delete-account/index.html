Delete your Wearly account

Wearly is made by Softilox Innovations Inc. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Wearly and go to You.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

By email

If you can't open the app, email support.wearly@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Wearly account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your profile and sign-in details, clothing photos, closet items and tags, outfits, events, trips, about-you details, usage history and scan credits are permanently deleted.

What may be kept

Records of purchases may be kept for up to 7 years where tax or accounting law requires. Anonymous, aggregated statistics that can't identify you may be kept.

Subscriptions are billed by Google Play: deleting your account doesn't cancel billing. Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
